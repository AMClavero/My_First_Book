# Solving quadratic equations

This example chapter shows how to solve quadratic equations of the form

$$ax^2 + bx + c = 0$$

Assuming $a \ne 0$, the solutions are given by the quadratic formula:

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

Quick example:

- For $x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0$ we have $a=1,b=-3,c=2$ → $x=1$ or $x=2$.
